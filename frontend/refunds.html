Refund Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

This policy covers payments for OrderFlow and OrderFlow RE plans.

Try before you pay

Every new account gets a 3-day free trial that starts when we approve your account. We encourage you to test the bot fully before paying.

How paid plans work

You pay a one-time setup fee and then a monthly service fee, both by bank transfer. Each monthly period lasts 30 days and does not renew automatically. When it ends, your bot pauses and you are not charged again unless you pay again.

When we refund

  • If you paid and the service did not work because of a fault on our side, tell us within 7 days of activation. If we cannot fix it within 72 hours, we will refund the monthly fee you paid for that period, and the setup fee if we could not complete your setup.
  • If we charged you twice or by mistake, we will refund the extra amount.
  • If WhatsApp restricts or bans your number within 7 days after your setup is completed, we will refund 50% of the setup fee.

When we do not

  • Time already used on a working service, and the setup fee once your setup has been completed.
  • Restrictions or bans applied to your number by WhatsApp after the first 7 days following setup, which are outside our control.
  • Accounts ended for breaking our Terms.

How to ask

Email emmanueljoshuaaa08@gmail.com with your business name and the transfer reference. Approved refunds are paid by bank transfer within 7 business days.
