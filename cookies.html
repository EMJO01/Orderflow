This page explains what OrderFlow stores in your browser.

What we use

  • Local storage, strictly necessary: when you log in, your browser keeps a login token and basic profile details so you stay signed in. They stay until you log out or clear your browser data.
  • Cookies: we do not currently set advertising or analytics cookies.

Third parties

Some pages load scripts or fonts from content delivery networks such as Cloudflare's cdnjs. These providers receive your IP address and browser details when the file loads.

Your choices

You can clear local storage and cookies in your browser settings. You will be signed out.

If this changes

If we add analytics or advertising tools, we will update this page and ask for your consent first.
