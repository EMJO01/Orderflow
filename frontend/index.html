FAQ
Common questions
Do my customers need to download anything?
No. Your customers just message your WhatsApp number like they always have. Nothing changes for them.
What happens to my existing WhatsApp chats?
Your chat history is preserved. We migrate your number to the WhatsApp Business API which is a standard process. Your contacts stay the same.
How long does setup take?
After you sign up, we set everything up within 48 hours. You add your products (takes about 10 minutes) and we handle the technical connection.
Can I use it for food, fashion, or any business?
Yes — OrderFlow works for any business that takes orders on WhatsApp. Food vendors, fashion stores, beauty products, electronics, groceries, and more.
What language does the AI reply in?
The AI automatically matches the language your customer writes in. English, German, French, Pidgin, Yoruba — it adapts naturally.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel anytime from your dashboard.
