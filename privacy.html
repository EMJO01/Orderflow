OrderFlow by Nexua

Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

This policy explains how Nexua ("we", "us") handles personal data in OrderFlow and OrderFlow RE. We aim to follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

Our two roles

For vendors and agents who sign up, we are the data controller of your account data. For your customers and prospects, the business that uses OrderFlow decides why their messages are used, and we process that data on the business's behalf. If you messaged a business and want your data corrected or removed, ask that business first. You can also email us and we will pass your request on.

What we collect

  • Account data: name, business name, email, WhatsApp number, country, business description, bot instructions and your password (stored hashed, never in plain text).
  • Catalogue and listings: products or properties, prices and descriptions.
  • Conversation data: phone number, name, messages, delivery address, budget and property preferences, orders, viewing requests and lead stage.
  • Connection data: the credentials that keep a vendor's WhatsApp connected, stored in our database so the bot stays online.
  • Technical data: a login token and profile in your browser's local storage, and standard server logs.

How we use it

To run the service, generate bot replies, send you alerts and account messages (such as approval and plan-expiry notices), keep the platform secure, and give support. We do not sell personal data.

AI processing

To write replies, message content and the vendor's catalogue or listings are sent to Anthropic, which provides the Claude AI model. We send only what is needed to produce the reply.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database), Render (server), Netlify (website), Anthropic (AI) and content delivery networks such as Cloudflare's cdnjs for page scripts. Some of these process data outside Nigeria, under their own security and data protection terms.

How long we keep data

  • Account data is kept while your account is open.
  • Orders, leads and viewings are kept until you delete them or ask us to.
  • The bot keeps only the last 10 messages of each chat in memory, and these clear when the server restarts.
  • We act on deletion requests within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, object to its use, or withdraw consent. Email [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]]. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

We hash passwords, restrict each account to its own data and use HTTPS. No online service is perfectly secure. Tell us at once if you think your account has been compromised.

Children

OrderFlow is for businesses and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell account holders.
