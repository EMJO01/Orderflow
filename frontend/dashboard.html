🧠 Train Your Bot
Tell your bot how to behave — your hours, policies, delivery info, or anything customers frequently ask. The bot will follow these rules in every conversation.
⏰ Availability "I'm unavailable from 2pm–5pm daily"
🚚 Delivery "Delivery takes 2-3 business days"
↩️ Returns "No refunds after 24 hours"
💰 Minimum Order "Minimum order amount applies"
💳 Payment "Bank transfer & cash on delivery"
📋 Order Details "Always collect name & address"
Changes take effect on the next customer message
💡 Tips for better bot training
1
Be specific — "I'm unavailable from 2pm to 5pm WAT" is better than "I'm sometimes busy"
2
Add your most common FAQs — delivery time, payment methods, return policy
3
You can update these anytime — the bot learns instantly on the next conversation
